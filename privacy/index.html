Privacy Policy

Effective July 5, 2026

The short version: Snailpocalypse has no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics, and no accounts. Your data lives on your phone and in your own iCloud. We never receive it — so we couldn’t sell it even if we wanted to.

Health data (Apple Health)

With your permission, Snailpocalypse reads your daily step totals from Apple Health (HealthKit). That’s what powers the game: your steps become distance, and the race against Toby is resolved on your device.

  • We only read step counts. We never write anything to Apple Health.
  • Your health data is never sent to us or to any third party, and is never used for advertising or marketing of any kind.
  • You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Snailpocalypse.

Game progress and iCloud

Your save (your run, your lead over Toby, your unlocked cosmetics) is stored on your device and synced through your personal iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit, so it survives phone upgrades. That storage belongs to your Apple Account — we cannot see into it.

If you use the friends feature, the friends you choose to add can see your display name and your race progress (things like your current lead and streak) — that’s what makes the feature work. You can remove friends at any time.

Purchases

Optional cosmetic items are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card number or billing details. Cosmetics never affect survival — distance can only be walked.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or trackers.
  • No accounts, emails, or passwords.
  • No location tracking and no access to your contacts.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data — we have nothing to sell.

Children

Snailpocalypse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included — the app simply isn’t built to send us data.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
