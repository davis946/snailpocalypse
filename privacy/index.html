Privacy Policy

Effective July 25, 2026

The short version: Snailpocalypse has no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics, and no accounts. The game runs on your phone and your save lives in your own iCloud. Nothing about you is published anywhere until you switch on a feature that shares it, and you can switch it back off.

Health data (Apple Health)

With your permission, Snailpocalypse reads your daily step totals from Apple Health (HealthKit). That’s what powers the game: your steps become distance, and the race against Toby is resolved on your device.

  • We only read step counts. We never write anything to Apple Health.
  • Your health data is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never handed to a data broker or any other third party.
  • Step data stays on your device unless you turn on one of the sharing features below, which publish a step total (never individual samples, never times or locations). Those features are off until you turn them on.
  • You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Snailpocalypse.

Game progress and iCloud

Your save (your run, your lead over Toby, your unlocked cosmetics) is stored on your device and synced through your personal iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit, so it survives phone upgrades. That storage belongs to your Apple Account — we cannot see into it.

The sharing features

The social parts of the game — friends, the world leaderboard, guilds, MegaToby raids, and visiting other yards — work through a shared area of the app’s iCloud (a CloudKit public database). This is separate from your private save. Anything published there can be seen by other players, and, unlike your private save, it is technically visible to the developer too.

Every one of these is off until you turn it on, and each publishes only what its feature needs:

  • Friends. Turns on when you share your friend code or add someone else’s. Publishes your display name, your current lead, your day, your run status, and your step total for the week. Anyone holding your code can see it. Turning it off in Settings → Health & privacy deletes what was published.
  • World leaderboard. A toggle on the leaderboard screen. Publishes your display name, lead, day, lifetime step total, snail skin, achievement count, and your device’s country setting (the region in your iOS settings, never your location). Visible to every player.
  • Guilds and raids. Joining one publishes your display name, your guild or team membership, and your step contribution to the other members.
  • Yards. Visiting, likes, and gifts publish your yard’s appearance and the gifts you send.

You can remove friends at any time, leave a guild or team, and switch any of these off.

Weather

If you set a city for the cosmetic sky, that city name is sent to Open-Meteo to look up the forecast. Only the city you typed is sent. The app never reads your location, and the weather never affects the race.

Purchases

Optional cosmetic items are sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment; we never see your card number or billing details. Cosmetics never affect survival — distance can only be walked.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or trackers.
  • No accounts, emails, or passwords.
  • No location tracking and no access to your contacts.
  • No selling or renting of your data, to anyone, ever. The only data that leaves your phone is what a sharing feature you switched on publishes to other players.

Children

Snailpocalypse is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The app has no accounts and asks for nothing about you; the only thing it can publish is a display name you choose yourself, and only if you switch on a sharing feature.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
