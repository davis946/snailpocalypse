Support

Questions, bugs, or a snail emergency

Email davistaang@gmail.com and a human will read it — Snailpocalypse is made by one person, so you’re writing straight to the developer.

Common questions

Toby didn’t move overnight — did something break?

Days resolve automatically when you open the app: one resolution for each calendar day that has passed, using your step totals from Apple Health. Just open the app and the missing days will play out in order. No button needed — there isn’t one.

The app can’t see my steps

Snailpocalypse needs permission to read steps from Apple Health. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Snailpocalypse and make sure Steps is turned on. Also make sure your iPhone (or Apple Watch) is actually counting steps in the Health app itself.

I bought a cosmetic and don’t see it

Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and restore automatically. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase, then reopen the Wardrobe. Still missing? Email us with roughly when you bought it and we’ll sort it out.

Does anything in the shop make the game easier?

No — and it never will. Everything for sale is cosmetic (colors, hats, trails, yard decorations). Distance can only be walked. That’s a design rule, not a pricing decision.

I’m in the beta (TestFlight) — how do I report something?

Take a screenshot in the app, tap the share button, and choose the TestFlight feedback option — or just email us. Both land in front of the developer.
